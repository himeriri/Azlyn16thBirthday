🎤 The Performance

Everything was going perfectly normally.

Until someone suddenly says the words you never wanted to hear:

"You're performing."

You freeze.

You look around, hoping someone will tell you that this is a joke.

Nobody does.

There are people watching. There is a microphone nearby. And somehow, your name is already on the list.

You have approximately five seconds to mentally prepare yourself.

Fortunately, two people are standing nearby.

Who would you rather have encouraging you?
