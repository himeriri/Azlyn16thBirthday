🌤️ The Free Afternoon

It's an ordinary school day.

You've survived your classes, your responsibilities, and whatever mysterious events happened earlier.

You check your schedule one last time.

Nothing.

No assignments. No meetings. No deadlines.

For once, the afternoon is completely yours.

You quietly celebrate your newfound freedom...

when suddenly, you hear two familiar voices.

Who are you spending your afternoon with?
