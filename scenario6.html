📚 The Impossible Assignment

You thought the week couldn't possibly get any more complicated.

You were wrong.

There is an assignment.

And it is due soon.

You stare at the instructions.

You read them once.

You read them again.

Somehow, they make even less sense the second time.

You begin writing something anyway.

Five minutes later, you realize that you have somehow made the situation worse.

Your last remaining option is to ask someone for help.

Luckily, two people are available.

Who are you asking to help with the assignment?
