🍜 The Lunch Decision

The morning has finally come to an end.

After surviving several hours of school, you realize something extremely important.

You're hungry.

Very hungry.

You open your bag and discover that you have absolutely no idea what you want to eat.

You consider making the decision yourself...

but then you remember that two people nearby have very different ideas about what lunch should look like.

One promises a fun meal. The other insists that you should probably make a sensible choice.

Who gets to decide your lunch?
