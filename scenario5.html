🌧️ The Rain

The school day is finally over.

You step outside, ready to go home.

The sky looks a little cloudy.

You think nothing of it.

Then it starts raining.

Not a gentle little drizzle, either.

The kind of rain that makes you question every decision that led you to leaving your umbrella at home.

You stand underneath a small covered area, watching the rain pour down.

You check your bag.

No umbrella.

Just when you're beginning to accept your fate, you notice that two people are nearby.

Either one could help you get home.

Who are you waiting out the rain with?
